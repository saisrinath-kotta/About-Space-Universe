Infectious Diseases

Chronic Diseases
Long-lasting conditions that persist for a year or more, requiring ongoing medical care or limiting daily activities.

Mental Health Disorders
Conditions affecting mood, thinking, and behavior, significantly impacting a person's daily life and relationships.
🧬 Genetic Disorders
Genetic Disorders

Cancer
